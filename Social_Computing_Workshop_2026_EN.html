What is social computing?

It starts with human interaction and uses computational methods to observe, analyse and understand society.

Start with familiar social phenomena.
Step into computational methods.

Social networks, collective behaviour, information diffusion, algorithmic influence and human-AI interaction are all part of social computing. Accessible lectures introduce the fundamentals, hands-on tutorials let you try real tools and methods, and team projects bring participants from different backgrounds together.

Good to know

New to the field?
Curiosity is enough to begin.

Discover a new field, try a new method, or bring your research to find feedback and collaborators.

Can I submit without a complete paper?

Yes. We welcome published or unpublished completed studies, work in progress, preliminary findings, research ideas and prototype concepts. Your abstract may describe the question, methods, current results and what you hope to discuss or develop during the workshop.

Can I join without a computing background or coding skills?

Yes. Social computing is inherently interdisciplinary: social analysis, theory, user research, design and communication all matter. You may join individually and find teammates based on shared interests and complementary skills. Practical sessions begin with concepts and basic operations.

What will happen over the three days?

You will begin with concepts and cases, try methods involving data collection, network analysis or LLMs, and work with a team to turn a social question into an initial proposal, visualisation or simple prototype. The emphasis is on learning and exploration; you do not need a complete project at the outset.

What follow-up opportunities are available for promising projects?

Projects with research potential may receive further mentor feedback on research design, implementation, evaluation and user studies. Mature work may later be submitted through the normal editorial and peer-review process to relevant venues such as the Journal of Social Computing or ACM Transactions on Social Computing.

Can I attend only the classes?

Audit places may be available if capacity permits; please state your preferred mode of participation in advance. Auditing is not the same as full workshop participation, and access to team projects, awards and follow-up support is not guaranteed. Please confirm reimbursement eligibility with your institution.

How do I register, and what are the Poster requirements?

Use the “Register / Submit a Poster” button to complete the form and indicate whether you are submitting a Poster. The Poster submission is limited to one A4 page excluding references, single-spaced, in 10-point font with 1-inch margins on all sides, and may contain at most one figure OR one table. Chinese and English submissions are accepted, with optional anonymity.
